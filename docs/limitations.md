# Known limitations

1. **Clinical feasibility failed.** Invariant creatinine and no vancomycin description matches make this dataset unsuitable for the intended clinical validation. Scale does not substitute for clinical signal.
2. **Patient parsing remains imperfect.** Original ingestion has 1,595,050 rows; repaired Parquet has 1,594,711. The original is retained in a pre-repair backup. A 339-row difference is verified, but record-level attribution is not established. Permissive parsing and null-padding do not certify fidelity.
3. **Demographic fields remain unsafe for substantive inference.** There are four repaired schema variants; 922 rows have nonempty `column23`. Historical QA reports generic columns and malformed demographic domains. A registry eligibility flag only handles absence/conflicting core identities; additional field-level validation is required.
4. **Identifiers are engineering keys.** The registry has 1,595,720 IDs, including 1,854 with no patient row and 43 held demographic identities. It is not a count of clinically adjudicated people. The 461 missing patient-ID rows cannot be reconciled by inventing identities.
5. **Historical patient counts differ by stage.** The original report's patient count and compression ratio remain valid descriptions of that stage, not repaired-state values. Row-count agreement alone is not proof of semantic or byte-level equivalence.
6. **Searches are literal.** `%creatinine%` and `%vancomycin%` preserve the original case-insensitive description logic. They do not provide comprehensive code-based phenotyping, unit harmonization, or clinical adjudication.
7. **Reproducibility is bounded.** Full registry/kill-test reruns succeed from current Parquet. Full raw conversion was not rerun; fixture conversion was. Original absolute paths broke after relocation; the CLI makes configuration explicit but generated views still reference absolute source paths.
8. **Benchmarks are historical local observations.** Metadata-assisted count queries, cache state, hardware, and the original environment were not controlled as a comparative benchmark. Do not generalize the recorded timings.
9. **Scientific gates are preserved, not clinically calibrated.** Thresholds are engineering rules. The historical empty-data handling and signature construction are documented in scientific scope and architecture; no clinical interpretation is warranted for arbitrary new inputs.
10. **Provenance gaps remain.** No independently timestamped prespecification, exact generator seed, download receipt, or archival DOI was recovered. These gaps are disclosed rather than invented.
